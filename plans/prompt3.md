### Research topic

**Can classical Riemann–Cartan geometry be consistently coupled to quantum matter without quantizing spacetime?**

### Investigate five questions

1. What has already been established about semiclassical Einstein–Cartan gravity coupled to quantum matter?
2. Can Oppenheim-style stochastic, completely positive classical–quantum dynamics be extended to independent tetrad and spin-connection variables?
3. How do quantum spin-current fluctuations interact with algebraically constrained torsion?
4. Can such a theory preserve local Lorentz invariance, diffeomorphism constraints, conservation laws and relativistic consistency?
5. What predictions or experimental observations could distinguish this framework from semiclassical Einstein–Cartan gravity and quantum Einstein–Cartan gravity?
