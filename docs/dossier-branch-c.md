## 1. Research question and scope

Can classical Riemann-Cartan geometry be consistently coupled to quantum matter
without quantizing spacetime, and what mathematical conditions must such a
theory satisfy? (I-002.) Regime: macroscopic to cosmological scales, quantum
effects perturbative; Planck-scale quantum gravity excluded. Three frameworks
kept separate throughout: (A) semiclassical EC, (B) stochastic/hybrid EC,
(C) quantum EC.

## 8. Competing hypotheses

- H-002: stochastic hybrid EC is constructible (proposed, unproven).
- H-003: semiclassical-sufficiency null — stochastic torsion is inconsistent
  (closure fails) or unnecessary (Cartan-suppressed); falsified only by a full
  O1–O7 derivation plus a distinct computed observable.
- Distinguisher: O3 (dynamical vs algebraic torsion) and O5/O6 (closure with
  torsion vs closure failure). One derivation attempt settles both records.

## 10. Research assessment

- Strongest result: P-006's Hadamard-renormalized semiclassical EC (Framework A
  works as mean-field theory) on the P-007 classical baseline.
- Most important unresolved problem: O5/O6 — whether CQ constraints close with
  torsion. It gates the entire Framework B program and decides H-002 vs H-003.
- Recommended next investigation: attempt the P-003 Dirac-argument extension
  to EC variables for the momentum constraint (O5) on paper; a clean failure
  (non-closure/over-constraining) is as valuable as success.
