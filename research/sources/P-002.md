## Classification

tangential — one-dimensional supersymmetric/stochastic methods (Fokker-Planck, drift potentials). Flat-space only; no gravity, torsion or spacetime geometry. Retained as mathematical background, not as a hybrid-dynamics foundation.

## Claims

- Supersymmetric method for conditionally exactly solvable quantum systems extended to classical stochastic systems with Fokker-Planck equations (locator: abstract, p. 1; introduction, p. 1).
- Construction of drift-potentials on real line and half-line for exactly solvable Fokker-Planck equations (locator: abstract; section 2, p. 2-3).
- Explicit drift potentials for mono-, bi-, meta-, and unstable systems with decay rates and modes in closed form (locator: abstract; sections 3-5, pp. 3-6).
- Supersymmetric transformation of Fokker-Planck equation to imaginary-time Schrödinger equation (locator: section 2, pp. 2-3; equation (2.1)-(2.5)).
- Does NOT formulate interaction between classical tetrad and quantum matter state (locator: entire text — no mention of tetrad, spin-connection, torsion, Einstein-Cartan, gravity, or spacetime geometry).
- Does NOT formulate interaction between classical gravitational variables and quantum matter (locator: entire text — flat-space quantum mechanics and Fokker-Planck only).

## Evidence boundary

This paper is a valuable mathematical reference for supersymmetric methods in one-dimensional stochastic dynamics (Fokker-Planck equations). It is NOT a foundational reference for Oppenheim-style classical-quantum gravity hybrid dynamics. Any proposed extension of its supersymmetric method to Cartan geometry (tetrad/spin-connection variables) requires an independent mathematical derivation that has NOT been performed in this investigation. This limitation is explicitly stated in I-002 and H-002 corrections.
