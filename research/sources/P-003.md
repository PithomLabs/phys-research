## Classification

directly relevant — studies the physical framework of classical gravity coupled to quantum fields with stochastic backreaction, including constraint algebra. Metric formulation only; no torsion.

## Claims

- Studies theories where spacetime is classical and interacts with quantum fields, linear in density matrix and phase-space density, circumventing no-go theorems and pathologies of semi-classical gravity (locator: abstract, p. 1).
- Theory can be fundamental or effective; classical limit of spacetime is taken; dynamics of GR is the classical limit (locator: abstract, p. 1).
- Invariant under spatial diffeomorphisms; methodology to derive constraint equations by imposing invariance under time-reparametrization (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4, p. 9).
- Generalisations of Hamiltonian and momentum constraints derived for CQ theories reproducing Einstein gravity (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 3.2, p. 12).
- Constraint algebra computed for "discrete class" of CQ theories with quantum scalar field interacting with gravity (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4, pp. 9-15).
- **Key finding**: Constraint algebra does not close without additional constraints for a wide class of realisations, though these do not necessarily reduce local degrees of freedom (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4.4, pp. 14-15).
- Classical-quantum (CQ) dynamics formalism developed: linear in density matrix and phase-space density (locator: section 2.1, pp. 4-8).
- Liouville formulation of classical gravity and CQ theories of gravity developed (locator: sections 3.1, 3.2, pp. 10-13).

## Evidence boundary

This paper is a **directly relevant** reference for the classical-quantum gravity framework (Oppenheim-style). It establishes the mathematical framework for classical gravity coupled to quantum fields with stochastic backreaction, and critically identifies that the constraint algebra does not close for a wide class of realisations without additional constraints. This is a core constraint-algebra obstruction that directly bears on the research question in I-002. The paper does not address Einstein-Cartan theory (torsion, independent tetrad/spin-connection) — it works in metric formulation of GR. Extension to Einstein-Cartan geometry is an open problem not addressed here.
