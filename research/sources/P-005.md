## Classification

directly relevant — derives the general form of hybrid classical-quantum dynamics under complete-positivity and probability-preservation; provides the mathematical classification of hybrid evolution. No gravity application in this paper.

## Claims

- Consistent hybrid classical-quantum dynamics exists iff it is stochastic; dynamics is linear in hybrid state, completely positive and trace-preserving (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 2, pp. 3-5).
- Application to back-reaction of quantum fields on space-time, avoiding semi-classical pathologies (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 1, p. 1-2).
- Toy models: qubit coupled to particle in potential; quantum harmonic oscillator coupled to classical one (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 3, pp. 5-8).
- Unravelling approach for calculating dynamics; numerical simulation code provided (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4, pp. 8-12).
- Trajectories (histories) of unravelling can be unique, conditioned on classical degrees of freedom for discrete realisations, when different jumps in classical DOF are accompanied by unique operators on quantum system (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4.1, pp. 8-10).
- Measurement postulate of quantum theory not needed; quantum systems become classical through interaction with fundamentally classical field (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 5, pp. 12-14).
- General form of continuous hybrid dynamics derived under complete-positivity and trace-preservation (locator: section 2, pp. 3-5; section 5, pp. 12-15).
- Memoryless continuous hybrid dynamics derived under complete-positivity and probability-preservation (locator: section 2, pp. 3-5; section 5, pp. 12-15).
- Toy models: qubit + particle in potential; quantum oscillator + classical oscillator (locator: section 3, pp. 5-8; appendix A).

## Mathematical content

- General Markovian CQ master equation, Eq. (4): d%(z,t)/dt = -i[H(z),%(z,t)] + Sum_ab Int dD W^ab(z|z-D) L_a %(z-D,t) L_b^dagger - (1/2) Sum_ab W^ab(z) {L_b^dagger L_a, %(z,t)}_+, with z the classical phase-space point and W^ab non-negative transition rates (locator: Eq. (4), section 2; status: general form derived in prior work [51,53], used and extended here).
- Normalization condition W^ab(z) = Int dD W^ab(z+D|z) preserving the quantum-state norm after tracing the classical sector, Eq. (5) (locator: Eq. (5), section 2; status: derived in source context).
- CP/TP/separation properties: Eq. (4) is completely positive over classical and quantum sectors, trace-preserving (Int dz Tr[%(z)] = 1), linear in the CQ state, and preserves the classical-quantum split (locator: section 2 discussion; status: proved in source).
- Trajectory uniqueness: unravelled trajectories are unique conditioned on the classical degrees of freedom for discrete realizations, when distinct classical jumps pair with unique Lindblad operators — classical monitoring reveals the quantum jump sequence (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4.1; status: derived in source).
- Toy-model master equation for qubit-plus-particle, Eq. (28), with diagonal Lindblad operators Eqs. (27)-(30), section 3; status: worked example in source).
- Regime: bounded Lindblad operators, finite-dimensional quantum systems coupled to classical particles/oscillators; Markovian on the full hybrid, non-Markovian on either reduced sector (locator: section 2; status: stated scope).

## Evidence boundary

This paper is a **directly relevant** reference for the mathematical structure of hybrid classical-quantum dynamics. It provides the general form of continuous hybrid dynamics under complete-positivity and probability-preservation, which is the mathematical foundation for any stochastic hybrid gravity theory (including I-002/H-002). It does NOT address gravity specifically — it develops the general mathematical framework for hybrid dynamics applicable to any classical-quantum system. The toy models are simple (qubit + particle; oscillator + oscillator) and do not include gravity, tetrads, spin-connections, or torsion. The paper provides the mathematical classification of hybrid evolution that any stochastic hybrid gravity theory must satisfy (complete positivity, trace-preservation, stochasticity).
