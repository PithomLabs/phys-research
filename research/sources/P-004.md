## Classification

directly relevant — studies stochastic metric modes in the postquantum theory of classical gravity, including metric fluctuations, stochastic gravitational-wave energy density and decoherence bounds. Metric formulation only; no torsion.

## Claims

- Studies fluctuations of the metric in the postquantum theory of classical gravity (classical spacetime coupled to quantum matter fields); mathematical consistency requires spacetime to evolve stochastically (locator: abstract, p. 1).
- Linearizes around Minkowski space; scalar-vector-tensor decomposition identifies stochastic modes: classical spin-2 field and spin-0 scalar, both diffusing around wave equations (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 3, pp. 5-8).
- Stochastic modes related to degrees of freedom in quadratic gravity but interpreted as stochastic contributions to spacetime (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4, pp. 9-10).
- Action is positive semi-definite (PSD) on all dynamical modes — necessary for consistent classical treatment of spacetime (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 3, pp. 5-8).
- Non-dynamical vector and scalar fields identified; Newtonian potential is non-dynamical scalar (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 4, pp. 9-10).
- Two-point function and power spectral density for Newtonian potential fluctuations computed; compared to LISA Pathfinder excess noise (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 5, pp. 12-15).
- Bounds on dimensionless coupling constants from LISA Pathfinder and stochastic gravitational wave energy density in FLRW background (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 6, pp. 15-18).
- Decoherence rate for table-top experiments depends on fluctuations in Newtonian potential Φ and curvature perturbation ψ (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 7, pp. 18-20).
- Consistency shown between Onsager-Machlup, Martin-Siggia-Rose, and path-integral formulations of pure gravity theory (locator: abstract, p. 1; section 8, pp. 20-22).

## Evidence boundary

This paper is a **directly relevant** reference for the postquantum theory of classical gravity. It explicitly studies stochastic metric fluctuations in the covariant theory coupling classical spacetime to quantum matter, identifying stochastic degrees of freedom (spin-2 and spin-0), computing power spectra, and comparing to experimental bounds (LISA Pathfinder). It operates in metric formulation of GR (not Einstein-Cartan). The extension to Einstein-Cartan geometry with independent tetrad and spin-connection variables is not addressed. The paper provides a working stochastic gravity theory with stochastic metric modes, which is directly relevant to the stochastic hybrid framework in I-002/H-002 but does not include torsion or independent spin-connection variables.
