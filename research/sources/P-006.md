## Evidence boundary

This paper is a **directly relevant** reference for semiclassical Einstein-Cartan gravity with torsion. It provides the first axiomatic semiclassical treatment of Einstein-Cartan theory with nonminimally coupled Klein-Gordon field, using Hadamard renormalization to obtain well-defined spin-density expectation values. It identifies renormalization ambiguities and constructs the renormalization Lagrangian in differential forms. This is the **only paper in this investigation that directly addresses semiclassical Einstein-Cartan gravity with torsion** — the others work in metric formulation (GR) or general hybrid dynamics without torsion. It does NOT include stochastic backreaction (it is semiclassical, not stochastic hybrid). The extension to stochastic hybrid dynamics with stochastic torsion is not addressed.
