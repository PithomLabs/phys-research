# Competing hypothesis: stochastic hybrid Einstein-Cartan gravity (proposed, unproven)

## Domain

Classical Einstein-Cartan geometry with independent tetrad `e^a_\mu` and spin-connection `\omega^{ab}_\mu`, in macroscopic regimes (solar-system to cosmological scales) where quantum effects are perturbative. The stochastic dynamics is proposed for the tetrad/spin-connection variables, not for spacetime points directly. The framework aims to describe how quantum-matter backreaction modifies the classical Cartan-geometry dynamics without quantizing the gravitational field.

**Status: PROPOSED, UNPROVEN CANDIDATE** — This hypothesis is a proposed framework requiring independent mathematical derivation. No derivation has been performed. Unsupported claims from the previous version have been removed.

## Premises

given tetrad_e spin_connection_omega
assume classical_cartan_geometry holds
assume quantum_matter exists

define tetrad_field e^a_\mu as the local orthonormal frame
define spin_connection \omega^{ab}_\mu as the independent Cartan connection
define torsion T^a_{\ \ \ \mu\nu} = \partial_\mu e^a_\nu - \partial_\nu e^a_\mu + \omega^a_{\ \ b\mu}e^b_\nu - \omega^a_{\ \ b\nu}e^b_\mu

## Missing mathematical ingredients (to be derived)

The following mathematical ingredients are **missing** and must be derived for this hypothesis to be established. They are NOT assumed. Labels O1-O7 match D-001 and the `?` lines under `## Open obligations`:

- **O1 — Stochastic spin-connection dynamics**: The spin-connection `\omega^{ab}_\mu` must have a stochastic evolution equation derived from a hybrid dynamics principle (per P-005 — complete positivity, trace-preservation, stochasticity). This has not been derived.

- **O2 — Stochastic spin-current source**: The stochastic evolution of the spin-connection must be sourced by a quantum spin-current operator (analogous to the energy-momentum tensor for the metric in P-004). No such source has been derived from the examined literature (P-003 through P-007). P-006 computes spin-density expectation values but not their stochastic backreaction on the connection.

- **O3 — Stochastic torsion evolution equation**: The torsion `T^a_{\ \ \ \mu\nu}` must satisfy a stochastic evolution equation. The function `f(e, \omega, quantum_backreaction)` in `T^a_{\mu\nu} = f(e, \omega, quantum_backreaction)` is **undefined** and must be derived from the stochastic spin-connection dynamics and the quantum spin-density expectation value.

- **O4 — Noise kernel for stochastic torsion**: The stochastic evolution of torsion requires a noise kernel specifying correlations of stochastic torsion fluctuations. No noise kernel has been derived. P-004 derives noise for metric fluctuations but not for torsion/spin-connection.

- **O5 — Generalized constraint with torsion (two classical branches; reformulated 2026-11-05, see Corrections)**: O5a (Blagojevic branch): the P-003 Dirac-argument method must be extended to EC variables with generators (Hperp, Ha, Hij) to obtain a generalized momentum constraint including torsion, with the Lorentz sector in the closure analysis. O5b (Kiriushcheva-Kuzmin branch): their gauge algebra is internal Poincare with no diffeomorphism constraint, so O5 restates as the translational-constraint problem — blocked upstream by their open D=4 algebra. Neither branch has been done. Background: derivations/O5/notes-2026-11-05.md Parts 1-2.

- **O6 — Constraint preservation / Hamiltonian constraint with torsion**: The torsionful constraint system must be shown preserved in time (P-003 Eq. (5.37) has no torsion analog; P-003 shows non-closure for metric CQ gravity without additional constraints or lapse/shift restrictions). O6 inherits O5's fork: preservation of the generalized Ha on the Blagojevic branch, of the translational constraint on the K&K branch. This has not been done on either branch.

- **O7 — Covariance, Lorentz invariance, CP/TP**: The stochastic evolution must preserve local Lorentz invariance and diffeomorphism covariance, and the EC hybrid dynamics must be completely positive and trace-preserving. None of this has been demonstrated.

## Falsification condition

A framework-specific experimental prediction is **not yet available**. The following conditions must be met before a falsification condition can be stated:

1. Derive stochastic torsion/spin-connection dynamics.
2. Derive the function relating stochastic dynamics to the Eötvös parameter: `\Delta\eta_{model} = F(stochastic coupling, spin density, matter composition, experimental geometry)`.
3. Declare a total-uncertainty prescription (stat + syst combination rule) and a multiplicity `N` (standard-deviation multiplier) **independently of the observed value** (addresses open F1 of R-001).

Only then can a falsification condition be stated as: `|eta_tipt_hat - \Delta\eta_{model}| > N * sigma_total` where `eta_tipt_hat` is the published MICROSCOPE estimate (P-001, Eq. (5), p. 5; corrected factor 2 in Eq. (1) from Plan 4 A).

**Current status**: No framework-specific prediction exists. MICROSCOPE is a WEP measurement and sensitivity benchmark only — it does not test the proposed stochastic Einstein-Cartan framework specifically (see E-002 correction).
