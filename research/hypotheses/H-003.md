# Semiclassical-sufficiency null for stochastic torsion

**Status: PROPOSED, UNPROVEN CANDIDATE** — competes with H-002. Claims that stochastic torsion is either inconsistent (constraint closure fails) or unnecessary (semiclassical EC suffices at accessible scales). No derivation performed; this record states what would settle the competition.

## Domain

Same regime as H-002: classical EC geometry (tetrad `e^a_\mu`, spin-connection `\omega^{ab}_\mu`) coupled to quantum matter, macroscopic to cosmological scales, quantum effects perturbative. The two hypotheses share the P-007 classical baseline and the P-006 semiclassical results; they differ on whether stochastic torsion dynamics (H-002 obligations O1-O7) exists and matters.

claim sufficiency: semiclassical EC (Framework A) accounts for all torsion effects at accessible scales
claim closure_risk: extending P-003 CQ constraints to torsion fails to close (O5/O6 unresolvable without over-constraining)
claim suppression: any residual stochastic torsion is Cartan-suppressed below detectability

## Falsification condition

H-003 is falsified (as a null) if BOTH of the following are exhibited:

1. A derivation resolving H-002 obligations O1-O7: stochastic spin-connection dynamics with an explicit spin-current source, noise kernel, closed (or consistently extended) constraint algebra, and verified covariance, local Lorentz invariance, complete positivity and trace preservation.
2. A computed observable consequence distinct from semiclassical EC plus P-004 metric stochasticity — e.g. a spin-composition-dependent free-fall deviation `Delta eta_torsion = G(couplings, spin density, geometry)` that is nonzero above a declared total-uncertainty prescription with multiplicity `N` declared independently of any observed value.

Falsification requires the derivation first: a bare experimental anomaly with no torsion model does not falsify H-003, it motivates one. Conversely, H-002 is weakened (not logically falsified) if O5/O6 are shown unresolvable, or if the only consistent resolution keeps torsion algebraic. The two hypotheses are distinguished mathematically by O3 (dynamical vs algebraic torsion) and O5/O6 (closure with torsion vs closure failure).

**Current status**: neither condition is met. No stochastic torsion dynamics exists (H-002 O1-O7 open); no torsion-specific observable has been computed under either hypothesis. MICROSCOPE (P-001) constrains WEP violation, not torsion, and benchmarks both hypotheses equally (see E-002).

## Evidential boundary

H-003 rests on: P-007 (algebraic torsion, Cartan suppression scale, vacuum equivalence with GR); P-006 (working semiclassical EC needing no stochasticity); P-003 (closure failure as a live risk for any CQ extension). It explains why no torsion signal has been seen (there is nothing to see at accessible scales) but explains nothing about quantum-gravity-scale physics, where semiclassical methods break down. What remains unexplained under H-003: whether P-005 CPTP dynamics admits ANY consistent EC realization (if yes, H-003's closure_risk claim fails even if suppression holds). The distinguishing calculation is O3/O5/O6, shared with H-002 — the competition is genuine: one derivation attempt settles both records.
