# The classical–quantum Einstein–Cartan extension: stochastic hybrid dynamics with independent tetrad and spin-connection variables

## Question

Can a consistent stochastic hybrid classical-quantum theory be constructed for Einstein-Cartan gravity with independent tetrad and spin-connection variables, without quantizing spacetime?

## Theoretical expectation

- Claim: A stochastic hybrid dynamics for Einstein-Cartan gravity with independent tetrad and spin-connection variables can be constructed by extending the Oppenheim-style postquantum classical gravity framework to include torsion and independent spin-connection variables.
- Assumptions: (1) Classical gravity (tetrad + spin-connection) interacts with quantum matter fields; (2) Dynamics is stochastic, linear in hybrid state, completely positive and trace-preserving (per P-005); (3) Quantum backreaction modifies both metric and torsion via energy-momentum and spin-density expectation values (per P-006); (4) Constraint algebra must close (per P-003 — closure fails without additional constraints); (5) Stochastic metric modes exist (per P-004); extension must include stochastic torsion/spin-connection modes.
- Applicable regime: Macroscopic gravity (solar-system to cosmological scales) where quantum effects are perturbative; not Planck-scale quantum gravity.
- Source references:
  - P-007 (Trautman 2006): foundational classical EC baseline — Sciama-Kibble equations, algebraic torsion, conservation laws, Cartan radius.
  - P-003 (Oppenheim-Weller-Davies 2022): CQ gravity framework, constraint algebra non-closure, constraint equations from time-reparametrization.
  - P-004 (Oppenheim 2023): Stochastic metric modes in postquantum gravity; spin-2 and spin-0 stochastic modes; PSD action requirement.
  - P-005 (Oppenheim et al. 2026): General form of continuous hybrid classical-quantum dynamics under complete-positivity and trace-preservation; stochasticity required for consistency.
  - P-006 (Morales-Cabrera-Bonder 2026): Semiclassical Einstein-Cartan with torsion; Hadamard renormalization of spin-density; renormalization ambiguities; conformal anomaly with torsion.
  - P-002 (Junker 1998): Tangential — supersymmetric method for 1D stochastic Fokker-Planck systems; NOT a gravity theory. Kept as tangential mathematical background only.

## Independent theoretical result

NOT APPLICABLE — no independent derivation of the full stochastic hybrid Einstein-Cartan theory has been performed. The primary literature (P-003, P-004, P-005, P-006) provides the core theoretical components but they have not been synthesized into a complete stochastic hybrid Einstein-Cartan theory. The constraint algebra non-closure (P-003), stochastic metric modes (P-004), general hybrid dynamics classification (P-005), and semiclassical Einstein-Cartan with torsion (P-006) are separate theoretical components that have not been synthesized into a complete stochastic hybrid Einstein-Cartan theory. This synthesis is the open problem.

## Experimental expectation

- Predicted observable: Stochastic torsion/spin-connection modifications to differential free-fall acceleration between test masses with different spin compositions, potentially detectable at sensitivities comparable to MICROSCOPE (P-001: arXiv 2209.15487v1, Eq. (5)).
- Units and conditions: Dimensionless Eötvös parameter η; statistical and systematic uncertainties must be declared separately; any combined total-uncertainty prescription must be explicitly declared with its coverage convention before comparison.
- Falsification: A statistically significant deviation from the null prediction under a declared total-uncertainty prescription, with the multiplicity N (number of standard deviations) declared independently of the observed value.
- Note: MICROSCOPE is a WEP measurement and sensitivity benchmark; it does not directly measure torsion, spin-current fluctuations, or stochastic spin-connection dynamics. Any model-specific prediction requires a derived equation connecting stochastic dynamics to the Eötvös parameter.

## Experimental actual

NOT APPLICABLE — no independent measurement of tetrad/spin-connection stochastic dynamics exists. The closest published measurement is MICROSCOPE (P-001), which measures differential free-fall acceleration but does not independently characterize the torsion budget, the independence between statistical and systematic terms, or an unmodelled-effects bound. See R-001 findings F4-F6 (systematic upper bound treated as σ, budget overreach, source transcription error with factor 2 — P-001 corrected in Plan 4 A, hypothesis half of F6 remains open in H-001).
