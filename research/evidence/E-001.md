# MICROSCOPE 2022 eta(Ti,Pt) measurement

## Measured value

The Eötvös parameter for the titanium/platinum test-mass pair, as published:

η(Ti, Pt) = [−1.5 ± 2.3 (stat) ± 1.5 (syst)] × 10⁻¹⁵

The reference instrument's null result, published alongside it:

η(Pt, Pt) = [0.0 ± 1.1 (stat) ± 2.3 (syst)] × 10⁻¹⁵

The paper reports no violation of the WEP from these values. It states that
the statistical error is given at 1σ, and that the measured approximated
Eötvös ratio δ was identified with the exact η in reporting the result.

## Units and uncertainty

Dimensionless (Eötvös ratio, a ratio of accelerations).

The two uncertainty terms are recorded separately, exactly as published:

- Statistical: 2.3 × 10⁻¹⁵ at 1σ for SUEP (Ti/Pt); 1.1 × 10⁻¹⁵ for SUREF (Pt/Pt).
- Systematic: reported as an upper bound of 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁵ for SUEP and 2.3 × 10⁻¹⁵ for SUREF, against a specification of 0.2 × 10⁻¹⁵.

No combined total uncertainty is published. Temperature variations are
identified as the main source of systematic error; the systematic term is an
upper bound rather than a characterized distribution, because temperature
variations during EP sessions were below the temperature probe's noise at
fEP. Combining the two terms (quadrature gives ≈ 2.7 × 10⁻¹⁵, linear sum
gives 3.8 × 10⁻¹⁵) is a declared choice made by whoever uses the number,
not a result of this measurement.

## Methodology

Differential electrostatic accelerometers (sensor units SUEP with a
Pt/Rh–Ti/Al/V pair, SUREF with a Pt/Rh–Pt/Rh pair) aboard the drag-free
MICROSCOPE satellite, in sun-synchronous dawn-dusk orbit, launched
25 April 2016 and ended 18 October 2018.

- Eighteen SUEP sessions and nine SUREF sessions used for the final result; a handful of earlier sessions discarded for non-linearities or anomalies.
- Iterative frequency-domain estimation with the Adam code, cross-checked against M-ECM (Modified-Expectation-Conditional-Maximization), which fills gaps by conditional expectation under a circulant covariance approximation.
- Short-lived "glitches" (probably multi-layer insulator crackles) masked by recursive σ-clipping: points deviating more than 4.5σ from the moving average and more than 3σ from the moving average of the high-frequency-filtered data, then one second before and 15 seconds after each outlier masked.
- Rare jumps in differential acceleration discarded by segmenting sessions between jumps; segments span an even number of orbital periods to cancel contamination at frequencies m·f_orb + n·f_spin.
- Off-centerings Δx, Δz estimated from their coupling with the Earth gravity gradient at 2f_EP; Δy from dedicated 5-orbit shaking sessions at f_cal.
- Thermal systematics characterized by dedicated heater sessions and a 30° tilt session over 465 orbits; temperature variations found attenuated by a factor 500 between the FEEU and the sensor unit, which was taken as the lowest limit for the higher-frequency bound.
- Mock WEP-violation signals added to the data to confirm the procedure does not suppress a real signal.
