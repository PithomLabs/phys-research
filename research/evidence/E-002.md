# Expected vs actual comparison: stochastic hybrid dynamics for Cartan geometry

## Measured value

No independent measurement exists. The closest published measurement is the MICROSCOPE differential free-fall parameter for Ti/Al/V vs Pt/Rh (P-001: Eq. (5), p. 5; corrected `eta_tipt` definition in Plan 4 A restores the factor 2 in Eq. (1)). The proposed stochastic framework (H-002) predicts a bounded torsion modification detectable at the same sensitivity regime, **but no model-specific prediction has been derived** (see H-002 correction). The comparison is conditional: the framework predicts a deviation only if the stochastic dynamics produces a non-zero effective `eta_tipt_hat` (the published estimate), which is compared to `sigma_total` using an explicitly declared prescription (see H-002 `## Falsification condition` — requires `N` declaration, addressing open F1 of R-001).

## Units and uncertainty

Dimensionless parameter `eta_tipt_hat` (the published estimate, distinct from the exact parameter `eta_tipt` = 0 by WEP construction — F2 of R-001 remains open in H-001 hypothesis half; F2 upheld in R-002; H-002 attempts to resolve by separating names but does not derive the stochastic dynamics mathematically). Uncertainty: statistical 2.3e-15 (1 sigma), systematic 1.5e-15 (upper bound, not standard deviation — F4 of R-001). Combined total declared by quadrature convention (2.7e-15); coverage convention not declared (F8). The hypothesis requires an explicit `define total_uncertainty_prescription` and `if |eta_tipt_hat| > N*sigma_total` with `N` declared independently of the data (F1 — remains open).

## Methodology

Expected-observable comparison method: compare the stochastic framework's predicted effective `eta_tipt_hat` (derived from tetrad/spin-connection fluctuations under the bounded-torsion assumption) against the published MICROSCOPE estimate (P-001). The comparison uses the corrected Eq. (1) definition (`2(aA - aB)/(aA + aB)`) from Plan 4 A and the declared quadrature total-uncertainty prescription acknowledged in H-002. The method is structural: it compares a proposed theoretical framework against a published experimental estimate, not an independent derivation or measurement.
