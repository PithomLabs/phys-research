## 14. Structural result: momentum-momentum does not close freely
(derived from Blagojevic (4.7) + P-003 framework; coefficients open)

Classical input, Blagojevic (4.7a):

  {Ha(x), Hb(y)} = [Ha' db + Hb da - 1/2 Rijab Hij] d(x-y)   (schematic)

The -1/2 Rijab Hij d term has NO metric-GR counterpart: momentum
generators close only modulo a Lorentz constraint with field-dependent
(curvature) coefficient.

CQ lift: La = {Ha,.} + Ca(%), with Ca the CQ corrections (matter
commutators + jump/no-event terms per A3'). Then

  [La(x), Lb(y)] = classical part + [Ca(x), {Hb,.}] + [{Ha,.}, Cb(y)]
                   + [Ca(x), Cb(y)].

The classical part reproduces (4.7a) including the curvature-Lorentz
term, which vanishes weakly (Lorentz gauge transformation of a
Lorentz-invariant observable). But the cross terms pair CQ jump
operators with the curvature coefficient Rijab(x): they have the
wrong transformation character (jump terms do not Poisson-transform
like {Ha,.}), producing anomalous terms with no classical counterpart
and nothing to cancel them within the (La, L) sector. Closure
therefore REQUIRES new transformation rules on the CQ couplings W,
coupling them to curvature — EC analogs of P-003 (5.13) arising ONE
COMMUTATOR EARLIER (at [La,Lb], where P-003 closes freely by
Assumption 2).

Consequence: on the Blagojevic branch, O5a's first deliverable is not
the momentum constraint itself but the [La,Lb]-closure transformation
rules; the generalized momentum constraint (from [L,L], P-003 §5.2.3
analog) can only be derived after those rules are imposed. The
Lorentz-sector commutators [Gij,La], [Gij,L] then constrain the
spin-current couplings — the same couplings O2 must construct
(O5a <-> O2 mutual dependency recorded).
