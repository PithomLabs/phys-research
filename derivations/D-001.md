# Framework comparison: semiclassical EC, stochastic hybrid EC, quantum EC

Title: Framework comparison — semiclassical EC, stochastic hybrid EC, quantum EC.
Scope: compare the three Branch C frameworks (I-002) against the examined sources. This record derives no new result; it organizes established components, transfer limits and open obligations. The seven obligations O1-O7 below match H-002's Phys-Pseudo `?` lines.

## Premises

- P-007: classical EC baseline — Sciama-Kibble equations, algebraic torsion, generalized conservation laws, Cartan radius.
- P-003: CQ gravity framework with classical metric + quantum fields; constraint algebra non-closure without additional constraints.
- P-004: Stochastic metric modes (spin-2 + spin-0) in postquantum gravity; PSD action requirement.
- P-005: General classification of continuous hybrid dynamics (CPTP, stochasticity required).
- P-006: Semiclassical Einstein-Cartan with torsion; spin-density expectation values; renormalization ambiguities; conformal anomaly with torsion.

## Dependencies

- P-007: classical EC field equations, conservation laws, effective-theory scale.
- P-003: CQ gravity framework, constraint algebra non-closure.
- P-004: Stochastic metric modes (spin-2 + spin-0) in postquantum gravity.
- P-005: General classification of continuous hybrid dynamics (CPTP, stochasticity required).
- P-006: Semiclassical Einstein-Cartan with torsion.

## Intermediate results

- Framework A (Semiclassical EC, P-006 on P-007 baseline): established semiclassical EC with torsion; no stochastic backreaction.
- Framework B (Stochastic hybrid EC): open problem; five established components not synthesized; obligations O1-O7.
- Framework C (Quantum EC): not addressed by any examined source.

## Mathematical rules

- Mathematical technique is not physical framework transfer: every transfer requires explicit variable mapping, equation correspondence, assumption reconciliation, and constraint verification.
- No cross-framework inheritance: results proved in one framework are not assumed in another.

## Obligations

- O1: Derive stochastic spin-connection dynamics for EC variables in P-005 CPTP form.
- O2: Derive the stochastic spin-current source (from P-006 semiclassical spin density to a stochastic source).
- O3: Derive the stochastic torsion evolution equation (beyond the algebraic Cartan relation).
- O4: Derive the noise kernel for stochastic torsion (P-004 gives metric noise only).
- O5: Extend the P-003 Dirac-argument method to EC variables: O5a generalized momentum constraint with torsion on the Blagojevic branch (Hperp, Ha, Hij); O5b translational-constraint restatement on the Kiriushcheva-Kuzmin branch (blocked by their open D=4 algebra).
- O6: Establish constraint preservation / the Hamiltonian constraint with stochastic torsion on either O5 branch (P-003 Eq. (5.37) has no torsion analog).
- O7: Verify covariance, local Lorentz invariance, complete positivity and trace preservation of the EC hybrid dynamics.

## A. Semiclassical Einstein-Cartan gravity (ESTABLISHED COMPONENT)

**Definition**: Classical EC geometry (tetrad + spin-connection) sourced by quantum-matter expectation values; mean-field backreaction, no stochasticity (P-006 on the P-007 baseline).

**Variables**: classical tetrad/coframe and metric-compatible connection (P-007 Eqs. (11)-(12)); quantum Klein-Gordon field (P-006 Eq. (21)).

**Field equations**: generalized Einstein Eq. (16) with tau_ab, Eq. (19); algebraic Cartan Eq. (17) with sigma, Eq. (20); matter Eq. (18)/(22) (P-006, section 2).

**Conservation**: generalized Bianchi-identity laws Dt, Ds (P-007 Eqs. (26)-(29)); energy-momentum divergence need not vanish with torsion.

**Established in source**: Hadamard bi-parametrix yields well-defined renormalized <tau> and <sigma>; renormalization Lagrangian in differential forms; scale and renormalization ambiguities identified; conformal anomaly persists with torsion, driven by the v1 term (P-006, sections 4-6).

**Model-dependent / unresolved**: nonminimal coupling xi not fixed by theory; renormalization ambiguities require physical input; no stochastic backreaction on metric or torsion; stochastic extension not addressed.

**Regime**: 4D, macroscopic and cosmological scales; torsion significant only below the Cartan radius (~10^-26 cm for the nucleon, P-007); classical geometry throughout.

**Transfer out**: the renormalized <sigma> is a candidate mean around which a stochastic spin-current source (O2) could be built — but P-006 gives no fluctuations, no noise kernel and no dynamics for the connection. Any use in Framework B requires O1-O4.

## B. Stochastic hybrid classical-quantum Einstein-Cartan gravity (OPEN)

**Definition**: Classical EC geometry with independent tetrad and spin-connection, coupled to quantum matter, with stochastic backreaction on BOTH metric and torsion/spin-connection. Dynamics must be stochastic, linear in the hybrid state, completely positive and trace-preserving (per P-005).

**Established components from examined sources**:

- P-007: classical EC variables, field equations, conservation laws, scale hierarchy.
- P-003: CQ gravity framework with classical metric + quantum fields; constraint algebra non-closure without additional constraints.
- P-004: Stochastic metric modes (spin-2 + spin-0) in postquantum gravity; PSD action requirement.
- P-005: General classification of continuous hybrid dynamics (CPTP, stochasticity required).
- P-006: Semiclassical Einstein-Cartan with torsion; spin-density expectation values; renormalization ambiguities; conformal anomaly with torsion.

**Open obligations**: O1-O7 above (identical to H-002's `?` lines). In particular: stochastic torsion/spin-connection dynamics NOT derived (P-004 derives stochastic metric modes only); stochastic spin-current source NOT derived (P-006 computes semiclassical expectation values only); noise kernel for torsion NOT derived; constraint closure with torsion NOT checked (P-003 shows non-closure for metric CQ gravity); covariance/Lorentz/CPTP for EC variables NOT verified.

**Transfer mapping (per the transfer rule)**:

- FROM P-003 (metric CQ constraints) TO EC: variable mapping MISSING — P-003's lapse/shift and metric variables have no EC counterpart stated; the Dirac-argument METHOD (Eqs. (4.18)-(4.23)) is the transferable item, not its metric results. Requires O5-O6.
- FROM P-004 (stochastic metric modes) TO EC: equation correspondence PARTIAL — SVT decomposition and PSD-action requirement apply to the metric sector; torsion/spin-connection modes absent. Requires O1, O3, O4.
- FROM P-005 (general CPTP hybrid form) TO EC: assumption reconciliation MISSING — Eq. (4) covers classical phase-space points + Lindblad operators; no mapping to connection variables or spin-current operators stated. Requires O1, O2, O7.
- FROM P-006 (semiclassical EC) TO stochastic EC: dynamics MISSING — mean-field sources with no fluctuations. Requires O1-O4.
- FROM P-002 (1D SUSY stochastic methods) TO EC: NO mapping exists; retained as tangential background only.

## C. Quantum Einstein-Cartan gravity

**Definition**: Quantized tetrad and spin-connection variables; gravity itself is quantized.

**Status**: Not addressed by any examined source (P-002 through P-007 all treat gravity classically).

**Transfer limitations**: Entirely different theoretical framework. Results from semiclassical or stochastic hybrid frameworks do not transfer without quantization (operator algebra, renormalization of quantum gravity) that is absent from Frameworks A and B. No transfer mapping is proposed; constructing one is out of scope for I-002.
