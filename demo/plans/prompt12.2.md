## 1. The operating model: autonomous execution with mandatory stop gates

| Situation                                  | Agent's authority                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal research and derivations            | Proceed autonomously                                       |
| Minor discrepancy                          | Record and continue                                        |
| Material uncertainty                       | Carry explicitly as conditional; continue independent work |
| Unresolved load-bearing assumption         | Stop dependent derivations; investigate independently      |
| Contradiction with primary literature      | Block affected branch and report                           |
| Missing or inaccessible primary evidence   | Record limitation; do not invent                           |
| Change of physical model or research scope | Require director approval                                  |
| Implementation or Phys-Pseudo work         | Not authorized                                             |

The important principle is that the agent should not need your permission to exercise scientific judgment, but it must not have permission to override scientific evidence.
