### 2. What I would change

The plan currently concentrates the formal Three Reality Checks application in Phase F. That is no longer sufficient.

Under RC-CONT-01, RC1, RC2 and RC3 must be applied throughout Phases 0–G, not merely consolidated near the end.

Here is how I would enforce that:

There is another important distinction: applying the checks must produce findings that change the investigation when necessary. It must not become a ritual in which an agent writes three paragraphs saying RC1, RC2 and RC3 passed.

For example, if Phase A discovers that an action coefficient is inconsistent with the cited source, Phase B cannot simply inherit that coefficient and continue. The discrepancy must be resolved or explicitly carried as an unresolved obligation.

## Continuous Application of the Three Mathematical Reality Checks — RC-CONT-01

The Three Mathematical Reality Checks are mandatory throughout the entire research investigation, beginning with planning and source acquisition. They are not reserved for Phase F, a final review, or a retrospective evaluation.

At every substantive research decision, claim, assumption, source interpretation, mathematical construction, derivation, approximation, transformation or cross-framework connection, the investigator must actively apply:

**RC1 — Mathematical Landscape**\
Identify the mathematical structures, their physical meanings, assumptions, applicability, validity regimes, limitations and excluded phenomena.

**RC2 — Mathematical Intersection**\
Examine how the relevant mathematical structures interact, what their intersection establishes, whether their assumptions are compatible, and what fails when a structure or assumption is removed or altered. Do not assert minimality without a removal or alteration argument.

**RC3 — Mathematical Interoperability**\
Examine every relevant mapping between mathematical frameworks, including source, target, mapping, assumptions, preserved properties, lost information, approximations, physical regime, proof obligations and failure conditions.

For each phase, record the consequential findings from all three checks. Findings must influence subsequent research decisions and derivations immediately. They must not be deferred to Phase F or Phase G.

Published papers are primary evidence of their authors' constructions and arguments, not unquestionable authorities. Their assumptions, derivations, mathematical compatibility and physical interpretations must be critically examined from first principles.

Distinguish definitions, assumptions, independently reconstructed derivations, imported results, approximations and empirical evidence.

No downstream derivation may silently inherit an assumption, coefficient, convention or mathematical relationship that an earlier phase has identified as questionable.

The selected RST–GSST model remains the director-approved research target. Continuous reality checks do not authorize an unnecessary reopening of model selection; they do require challenging the physical and mathematical premises of that selection. If a load-bearing contradiction is discovered, report it and apply the existing BLOCKED/CONDITIONAL decision rules rather than protecting the selected model.

A final successful review cannot retroactively legitimize an unsupported assumption or invalid mathematical bridge from an earlier phase.
