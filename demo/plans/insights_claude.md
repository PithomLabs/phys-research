If I had to depart from the six assumptions the last document discarded, I'd drop these three instead.

**1. Local regions have their own observables.** With diffeomorphism invariance, they don't. Invariant observables exist only relative to a dressing, such as a clock or reference system.

**2. The observer sits outside the system.** Time and measurement both need an internal reference. So the observer's clock belongs inside the algebra.

**3. Entropy is a given quantity.** For local QFT regions the algebra is type III. It has no trace, so entanglement entropy is regulator-dependent. Gravity may be what makes entropy well defined.

**The reformulation I'd run (call it ALG)**

- **Q1 (observables).** Take the invariant algebra built as the crossed product of the QFT algebra with the dressing group, including an observer clock whose energy is bounded below. This follows Witten (2021) and Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten (2022).
- **Q2 (entropy).** In that construction the algebra is type II, so it carries a trace. A trace is a noncommutative normalized measure. Entropy exists because the algebra has a trace, and the generalized-entropy formula is recovered up to a constant (Witten, Jensen–Sorce–Speranza).
- **Q3 (probability).** Gleason-type theorems extend to von Neumann algebras with no I₂ summand (Christensen, Yeadon). Types II and III have none, so the qubit exception drops out. The outcome problem is untouched.
- **Q4 (time).** Evolution is conditioning on the internal clock, the Page–Wootters structure in operator-algebra form.
- **Q5 (the metric).** The classical metric is the label of a semiclassical sector in the G_N→0 expansion. It is not an unquantized field.

This isn't BHC. It denies P3 (classical, unquantized g) and keeps gravity quantum perturbatively.

**Math reality check**
- **Derived from:** modular theory, crossed products, and trace existence. The intersection is algebra ∩ gravitational dressing ∩ trace, and here the "intersection principle" applies in a non-circular way.
- **Silent on:** outcomes, the value of Λ, and non-perturbative gravity. It is a G_N expansion, so every result carries that restriction.
- **Falsifiers:** (i) different clock choices giving non-isomorphic algebras would refute clock-independence. (ii) If the type-II conclusion depended delicately on how the observer's energy bound is imposed, that would be a model-dependence flag. I'm not sure about this point and would test it. (iii) De Sitter has no boundary dual, so nothing here ties R4 to AdS/CFT.

**A tension for your Bohmian focus.** Bohmian mechanics needs a preferred beable and a configuration space. No such structure is known on a type II or III algebra, which has no pure states or minimal projections. So P1 may not attach to ALG at all. If it doesn't, ALG is interpretation-neutral and R3's Bohmian pilot would be BLOCKED on that obstruction. That would be a genuine finding, and it is the strongest argument for running both cores side by side.
