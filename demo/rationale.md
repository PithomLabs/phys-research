The central question:

How does including the island contribution change the predicted entropy of Hawking radiation over time?

We start with the generalized entropy:

\\[ S\_{\mathrm{gen}}(R\cup I) = S\_{\mathrm{grav}}(\partial I) + S\_{\mathrm{matter}}(R\cup I). \\]

In JT gravity, the gravitational contribution is represented by the appropriate dilaton term, together with the model's topological contribution.

The radiation entropy is determined by comparing the extremized candidate branches:

\\[ S(R_t)= \min\left\\{ S\_{\mathrm{no\ island}}(R_t), \operatorname{ext}\_{\partial I} S\_{\mathrm{gen}}(R_t\cup I) \right\\}. \\]
